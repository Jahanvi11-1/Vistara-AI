RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") made this 1st day of November, 2023, is between PROPERTY MANAGEMENT LLC ("Landlord"), and JANE DOE ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES

Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the premises located at 123 Main Street, Apt 4B, Cityville, State 12345 ("Premises"), for use as a residential dwelling only.

2. TERM

The term of this lease shall commence on November 1, 2023, and shall terminate on October 31, 2024. If either party fails to provide a 60-day written notice of intent to terminate or modify this Agreement prior to the expiration date, this Agreement shall automatically renew for an additional 12-month period under the same terms and conditions, subject to a 15% increase in base rent. warning High Risk: Auto-Renewal

3. RENT

Tenant agrees to pay Landlord as base rent the sum of $2,500.00 per month, due and payable monthly in advance on the 1st day of each month during the term of this Agreement.

4. LATE CHARGES

If any amount under this Agreement is more than three (3) days late, Tenant agrees to pay a late fee of $150.00, plus an additional $25.00 for each day thereafter that the rent remains unpaid. info Medium Risk: Aggressive Late Fee

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $2,500.00 as a security deposit to secure Tenant's faithful performance of the provisions hereof.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR

Tenant shall keep and maintain the Premises in good and sanitary condition during the term of this Agreement. Tenant shall immediately notify Landlord of any damage or required repairs. article Low Risk: Standard Maintenance
