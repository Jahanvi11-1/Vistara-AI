High Risk

Auto-Renewal

Page 4, Section 12.b

Original Clause

"Upon expiration of the initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months each, unless either party provides written notice of termination to the other party no less than sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current Term."

translate Plain English

Your lease will automatically extend for another full year unless you tell them in writing 2 months before it ends.

Why It Matters

You can easily be locked into a binding 12-month contract if you miss the specific 60-day notification window.

Scenario

You plan to move out in July. You forget to send notice by May 1st. You are now legally required to pay rent until next July.

High Risk

Early Termination Penalties

Page 6, Section 18

Original Clause

"Tenant may terminate this Lease prior to the Expiration Date provided Tenant pays a termination fee equal to three (3) months Base Rent, plus forfeits the Security Deposit in its entirety as liquidated damages."

translate Plain English

Breaking your lease early will cost you 3 months of rent, plus they keep your entire security deposit.

Why It Matters

This is a significantly higher penalty than the state average (usually 1-2 months) and represents a severe financial risk.

Scenario

You get a new job in another city and need to break the lease 4 months early. You will have to pay thousands of dollars in fees.

Medium Risk

Broad Liability Waiver

Page 8, Section 22.a

Original Clause

"Landlord shall not be liable for any damage or injury to Tenant, or any other person, or to any property, occurring on the Premises or any part thereof, regardless of the cause..."

translate Plain English

The landlord claims they are not responsible for any injuries or damage that happen in your apartment, even if it's their fault.

Why It Matters

While often unenforceable in court if the landlord is negligent, it attempts to discourage you from seeking compensation for legitimate damages.

Scenario

A poorly maintained pipe bursts, ruining your electronics. The landlord may point to this clause to avoid paying for the damage.
